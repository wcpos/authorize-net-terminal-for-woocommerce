# Authorize.net Tap to Pay for WCPOS 0.1.0 (beta)

This is the first release, and it is a beta.

- The POS sends the amount to Authorize.net Tap to Pay. You take the payment in Authorize.net's own mobile app (US Tap to Pay on iPhone or Android). The POS finds the payment, records it once and completes the order.
- Before the nightly settlement, a refund is a full void. After settlement, a refund goes back to the card.
- Needs WooCommerce, WCPOS Pro 2.0 or later, and the Transaction Details API turned on in Authorize.net's Merchant Interface.

What we have and have not proven:

- Proven against Authorize.net's sandbox: sales, matching, void, and refund after settlement.
- A real tap in the Authorize.net app on a phone has not yet been proven by us. Please report how it goes, with the order number, the sale time and a screenshot.
- No physical card terminals, no in-app tips and no non-US accounts.
